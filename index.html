How a car moves through the shop.

Four stages, same order every time, whether the job is a brake fluid flush or a full concours preparation.

  1. 01

    Intake

    Every car is photographed panel by panel, measured for paint depth, and road-tested before anyone quotes a number. You get the photographs whether or not you book the work.

  2. 02

    Specification

    A written scope, fixed price and a firm collection date. If we find something mid-job, work stops and you get a call before the next hour is billed.

  3. 03

    Execution

    One lead technician owns your car start to finish. Paint depth is re-measured after every correction stage, and torque figures are logged against the manufacturer spec.

  4. 04

    Handover

    The car goes back to you under shop lights with the before-and-after readings, the parts we replaced, and a care sheet for whatever we put on the paint.
