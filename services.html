01

Paint & detail

Correction is measurement, not polish. We record paint depth on every panel before a pad touches the car, and again when it comes off, so you can see what was removed rather than take our word for it.

Correction
Up to three machine stages, depth logged before and after.
Coating
9H ceramic, nine-year rating, two-week cure window.
Interior
Steam extraction, leather re-conditioning, ozone treatment.

A full correction takes three to five days because the coating has to cure under controlled humidity. We will not rush that part, and we will not book your collection before it is ready.

From $890

Book paint & detail

02

Mechanical

Factory-level diagnostics for German and Japanese marques, scheduled servicing, and the suspension and brake work that most detail shops send down the road.

Diagnostics
Manufacturer scan tools, not a generic code reader.
Servicing
Oil, filters and fluids to the manufacturer schedule.
Brakes & suspension
Pads, discs, coilovers, geometry set on a Hunter rack.

Torque figures are logged against the manufacturer spec and go into the handover sheet. If we find something that was not on the original scope, work stops and you get a call before the next hour is billed.

From $140

Book mechanical

03

Glass & tint

Multi-layer ceramic film, plotted from a pattern cut to your exact model. No metal in the film, so nothing interferes with your keys, your phone or your radio.

Rejection
96% infrared, 99% ultraviolet, at every shade.
Cut
Plotter-cut patterns. No blade goes near your glass.
Warranty
Lifetime against purpling, bubbling and delamination.

Oregon allows 35% light transmission on front side windows and any darkness behind the driver. We will tell you what is legal before we quote, and we will not fit anything that is not.

From $420

Book glass & tint

Coverage

Onboard

Stays in the bays
